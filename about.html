The photographer

About

Hello, I’m Karina.

I fell in love with photography in my grandmother’s apartment in Kadıköy, flipping through old black-and-white wedding albums where every picture still felt alive. Twelve years later, I photograph around thirty weddings a year across Istanbul, Cappadocia and the Aegean coast.

My approach is calm and quiet. I watch, I wait, and I catch the moments you didn’t know were happening. I shoot only in black and white because it strips everything back to emotion, light and form — the things that will still matter when you look at these images in fifty years.

“She didn’t just photograph our wedding — she captured how it felt.”
Elif & Can · Married in Istanbul, 2025
